Frequently asked questions

Does this work on any PDF?

It works on PDFs that already contain fillable AcroForm fields. A plain scanned document with no form fields can't be filled this way.

Can I lock the answers in afterward?

Yes, use Flatten PDF after filling to make the entered values permanent and non-editable.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No, the form is filled entirely in your browser.
