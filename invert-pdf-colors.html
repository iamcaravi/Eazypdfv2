Frequently asked questions

Will this make my PDF look like dark mode?

It inverts every page's colors into a negative palette, which gives a dark-background effect similar to dark mode, though results vary by document.

Does this work well on text-heavy documents?

It works best on simple text documents. Documents with photos or colored graphics will also have those colors inverted, which may look unusual.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No, color inversion happens locally in your browser.
