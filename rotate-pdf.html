Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just one page instead of the whole document?

Yes, select individual pages in the page grid and rotate only those, independently of the rest.

What rotation angles are supported?

90°, 180°, and 270° in either direction, per page or applied to all pages at once.

Will rotating reduce PDF quality?

No, the page content itself is untouched — only its orientation metadata changes.
