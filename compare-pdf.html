Frequently asked questions

Does this compare formatting or just text?

The comparison is based on extracted text content — it highlights text differences between the two files, not visual/formatting changes.

Are both files uploaded to a server to compare them?

No. Both PDFs are read and compared entirely in your browser.

Can I compare scanned PDFs?

Only if the scan has an extractable text layer (e.g. it was OCR'd). Pure image scans with no embedded text can't be text-compared.
