Frequently asked questions

What kind of HTML is supported?

Basic text and simple formatting — this is not a full browser rendering engine, so complex layouts, CSS, and scripts won't be reproduced.

Can I convert plain text instead of HTML?

Yes, plain text is also accepted and laid out as a formatted PDF.

Is my content uploaded anywhere?

No, the PDF is built entirely in your browser.
