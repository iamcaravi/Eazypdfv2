Frequently asked questions

Will tables convert perfectly?

The converter approximates table structure from text spacing and position — it works well for simple tables but won't perfectly reconstruct complex, merged-cell layouts.

What happens to pages with charts or scanned images?

Pages with little extractable text are embedded as images in the spreadsheet instead, so nothing is lost.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Extraction and spreadsheet-building both happen locally in your browser.
