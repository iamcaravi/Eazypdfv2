Why use Merge PDF?

Merging comes up whenever a single PDF needs to hold what started as several separate files.

One file to send instead of many

Attaching five separate scans to an email invites at least one of them to get lost or opened out of order. One merged PDF removes that risk entirely.

Reports built from separate sections

When a report is drafted in pieces — cover page, chapters, appendix — merging is how those pieces become the single document you actually submit.

Scanned pages that arrived as separate files

A scanner or phone scanning app often saves each page as its own PDF. Merge PDF turns that pile back into one ordered document.

How it works

Upload

Add two or more PDF files from your device. Nothing is sent anywhere at this step.

Arrange

Drag the files into the exact order you want them to appear in the final document.

Merge

pdf-lib combines every page, in order, into a single new PDF — entirely in your browser.

Download

Save the merged file to your device. Your original files are untouched.

Frequently asked questions

Does merging PDFs upload my files anywhere?

No. Merge PDF runs entirely in your browser using pdf-lib — your files never leave your device.

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

No artificial limit. The only constraint is your browser's available memory for very large batches.

Can I reorder pages before merging?

Yes, drag files into the order you want before combining them into the final PDF.
