Frequently asked questions

Can I apply the same crop to every page?

Yes, set the crop box once and apply it across the whole document, or crop individual pages differently.

Can I zoom in for a precise crop?

Yes, the crop workspace supports zoom and page-by-page navigation for accuracy.

Is cropping reversible?

Cropping changes the page's visible area in the output file; keep your original PDF if you may need the untrimmed version later.
