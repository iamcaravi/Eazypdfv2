Frequently asked questions

How is this different from Split PDF?

Extract Pages keeps only the pages you pick into one new file; Split PDF divides the whole document into multiple output files.

Can I extract non-consecutive pages into one file?

Yes, select any set of pages and they're combined, in order, into a single extracted PDF.

Does this work on scanned PDFs?

Yes, extraction works on any valid PDF, including scanned documents, since it copies pages rather than reading text.
