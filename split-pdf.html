Why use Split PDF?

Splitting matters whenever a document is too long, too mixed, or only part of it is actually needed.

Only part of a document is relevant

A 40-page contract might only need one clause shared — split it out instead of sending the whole file.

One PDF holds several unrelated documents

Scanned batches often bundle multiple documents into one file. Splitting separates them back into individual files.

Print requires specific pages only

Printing a subset of a large PDF is easier once those pages exist as their own file.

How it works

Upload

Add the PDF you want to split. Nothing is sent anywhere at this step.

Choose a range

Pick a page range, or choose to split every page into its own file.

Split

Download

Get a single file or a ZIP of every split file, depending on your choice.

Frequently asked questions

Yes, choose exact page ranges or split every page into its own file.

Do I get the split files as separate downloads?

Yes, output files are bundled into a ZIP for download when splitting into more than one file.

Is my file processed on a server?

No, splitting happens locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
