Frequently asked questions

Can I combine multiple images into one PDF?

Yes. Add as many images as you like and they're combined into one PDF, one image per page, in the order you added them.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG and PNG images can be converted into a PDF.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is built locally in your browser using pdf-lib — your images never leave your device.
