Why use Edit PDF?

Editing is what's needed whenever a PDF's actual content — not just its pages — has to change.

Fixing a typo doesn't need the original file

A small correction in a scanned or finalized PDF shouldn't require going back to source software you may not have anymore.

Forms need filling, not printing and scanning

Typing directly into a PDF form is faster and cleaner than the print-sign-scan cycle.

Feedback belongs on the document itself

Annotating a PDF directly keeps comments attached to the exact page and line they refer to.
