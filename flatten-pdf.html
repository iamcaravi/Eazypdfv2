Frequently asked questions

What does flattening actually do?

It converts any fillable form fields into permanent, non-editable page content — the visible values stay, but they can no longer be typed into or changed.

Should I flatten before or after filling a form?

Flatten after filling the form (see Fill PDF Form) once you're ready to lock the answers in.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No, flattening happens locally using pdf-lib.
