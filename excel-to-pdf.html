Frequently asked questions

Which file types can I convert?

XLSX, XLS, and CSV files can all be converted into a table-layout PDF.

Are multiple sheets and cell formatting preserved?

This is a basic converter: only the first sheet is converted, laid out as a simple table. Cell colors, fonts, and multi-sheet workbooks are not preserved.

Is my spreadsheet uploaded anywhere?

No. It's read and converted entirely in your browser.
