Frequently asked questions

Does this extract embedded images or full pages?

Each full page is rendered as an image — this captures everything visible on the page, not just embedded photos within it.

What format are the extracted images?

Images are exported as PNG files, bundled into a ZIP for download.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Pages are rendered to images locally using pdf.js.
