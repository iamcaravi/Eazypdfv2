Frequently asked questions

What kinds of damage can this fix?

It can often recover PDFs with minor structural corruption by re-parsing and re-saving the document. Severely corrupted or truncated files may not be recoverable.

Is there a guarantee my file will be fixed?

No tool can guarantee recovery of every damaged file — if the underlying data is lost, repair can't restore it.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No, the repair attempt happens entirely in your browser.
