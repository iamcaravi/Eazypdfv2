Frequently asked questions

Is this a legally binding e-signature service?

No — this is a simple visual signature tool for adding a drawn signature to a document, not a certified e-signature platform with identity verification or audit trails.

Can I use a touchscreen or trackpad to sign?

Yes, the signature pad works with mouse, touchscreen, and stylus input.

Is my document uploaded to sign it?

No. The signature is drawn and placed onto the PDF entirely in your browser.
