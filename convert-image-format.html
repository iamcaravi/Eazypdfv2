Frequently asked questions

Which formats can I convert between?

PNG, JPG, and WebP are all supported as both source and target formats.

Does converting to JPG lose transparency?

Yes, JPG doesn't support transparency — converting a transparent PNG to JPG will fill the transparent areas with a solid background.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No, conversion happens locally in your browser.
