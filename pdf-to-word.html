Frequently asked questions

Is PDF to Word available now?

Yes. PDF to Word runs entirely in your browser — extracted text and images are laid out into a downloadable .docx file, no upload required.

Will the converted file be fully editable?

Text is extracted as editable content; pages with little extractable text (scans, image-heavy pages) are inserted as images instead, so nothing is lost.

Is my file uploaded to convert it?

No. Conversion runs in your browser using pdf.js and pdf-lib, with no file upload to any server.
