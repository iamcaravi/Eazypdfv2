Frequently asked questions

What does color inversion do exactly?

It flips every pixel's color to its negative — light areas become dark and colors shift to their opposites.

Which image formats are supported?

Common formats like JPG and PNG are supported.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No, inversion happens locally in your browser.
