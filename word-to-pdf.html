Frequently asked questions

Is my document uploaded to convert it?

No. The conversion reads your .docx file and builds the PDF entirely in your browser using mammoth.js and pdf-lib — nothing is sent to a server.

Will formatting be preserved exactly?

This is a basic, text-focused converter: it lays out the extracted text as a PDF. Complex formatting, images, and page layout from the original .docx are not preserved.

Do I need Microsoft Word installed?

No. Everything runs in the browser tab — no Word, no other software required.
