Frequently asked questions

Do I get one image per page?

Yes. Every page is rendered as a separate JPG image, bundled together into a ZIP file for download.

Is the PDF uploaded to convert it?

No. Pages are rendered to images locally in your browser using pdf.js — nothing leaves your device.

What resolution are the JPGs?

Pages render at a resolution suited for on-screen viewing and sharing — not designed for large-format printing.
